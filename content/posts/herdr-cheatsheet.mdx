# The Herdr Cheatsheet

[Herdr](https://herdr.dev) is a mouse-first terminal workspace manager — approachable for newcomers, familiar to tmux and zellij users. It keeps real terminal processes running, lets you detach without stopping your agents, and adds structure around your work.

The mouse covers everything, so keyboard bindings are optional. When you do use them, the default prefix is `ctrl+b` — shown below as <kbd className="ldr">C-b</kbd>. Press <kbd className="ldr">C-b</kbd><kbd>?</kbd> anytime to see every active binding.
