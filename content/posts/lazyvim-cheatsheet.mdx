# The LazyVim Cheatsheet

A companion to [my post on why LazyVim is my Neovim distribution of choice](/blog/lazyvim) — the keymaps worth memorizing, grouped by what you're trying to do.

The leader key is `<Space>` (written `⎵` on the keys below); the local leader is `\`. Tap `⎵` and wait, and [which-key](https://github.com/folke/which-key.nvim) shows every binding in that namespace.
